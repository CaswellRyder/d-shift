Find_Target lab kit

Your laptop opens the console. The Pi runs the camera, detection, tracking and estimated coordinates.

Start on your Mac laptop

  1. Extract and keep this entire folder together.
  2. Connect the configured Pi Zero W through its USB data port and let it boot.
  3. In Finder, double-click Verify Package.command, then Start Flight Lab.command.
  4. Enter the Pi SSH password in Terminal if asked. The live console opens at 127.0.0.1:8770.

Requires Python 3.9+, macOS SSH and a browser. No laptop pip packages or original computer paths are needed.

Setup, recording, calibration and download instructions

Capture and review

Choose balloon or goal, enter the measured lens-to-target distance, and press Capture 5 seconds. Preview pauses during recording. Frames and live results buffer before masks, overlays and downloadable files are generated.

Use Download Captures.command to save listed completed takes to this folder's Captures directory. The viewer currently lists the latest 100 takes.

The coordinate convention is X right, Y up, Z forward. A centred target 3 m ahead is (0, 0, 3) with an aligned camera.
